## The name

**RIVER** stands for **Raft-Integrated Validated Event Runtime**, the pipeline runtime the
project is named after. It is planned and has no code yet ([roadmap]({{< relref "roadmap" >}})).
In prose the project is always **Runink River**, never a bare "River": that name belongs
to an unrelated Wayland compositor.

## The logo

![The Runink River logo: a brown, white and tan puppy with a glowing cyan cube in its mouth](../../brand/river-mark.svg)

![The small form of the logo, the puppy's head, for 24 pixels and below](../../brand/river-mark-small.svg)

The Runink River logo is **the Runink mascot**, the mascot for Runink River and the people
who build on it: a brown, white and tan puppy with a glowing cyan cube in its mouth, drawn as
a sticker with a cream outline. Its dark outline, cream border and fills carry it on light and
dark grounds alike, so it needs no badge or background of its own (on a light ground the cream
border fades into the page and the dark outline holds the shape). The same drawing is the mark
on every surface of Runink River: the boot menu, the boot and sign-in screens, the desktop,
the icons and the terminal greeting. At 24 pixels and below, where the whole puppy closes up,
the small form frames just its head and the cube. Leave clear space around the logo of at
least a quarter of its width; never recolour or stretch it, and use the small form rather than
a crop of your own. The logo is raster art, so use it at 384 pixels or smaller, or take the
768 pixel PNG below for larger sizes.

## Colours

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#212121` |
| Surfaces | `#2C2E30`, `#36383A` |
| Text | `#E4E2D8`, secondary `#C6C7B5` |
| Accent (the one action colour) | `#C0CC7C` |

Headlines and the tagline are set in Playfair Display Italic, text in Figtree, code in Fira
Code (all SIL Open Font License 1.1).
