## The name

**RIVER** stands for **Raft-Integrated Validated Event Runtime**, the pipeline runtime the
project is named after ([runtime/README.md](https://github.com/org-runink/river/blob/main/runtime/README.md)).
In prose the project is always **Runink River**, never a bare "River": that name belongs
to an unrelated Wayland compositor.

The Runink River logo is **the Runink River dog**, the mascot for Runink River and the people
who build on it: the Runink herding dog as a brown-and-white puppy, grinning over the front
of a three-log raft on its way down the river. Its dark outline and cream and brown fills
carry it on light and dark grounds alike, so it needs no badge or background of its own. The
same drawing is the mark on every surface of Runink River: the boot menu, the boot and
sign-in screens (where the raft bobs on the water), the desktop, the icons and the terminal
greeting. At 24 pixels and below, where the whole puppy closes up, the small form frames just
its head. Leave clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of its width; never
recolour or stretch it, and use the small form rather than a crop of your own.
