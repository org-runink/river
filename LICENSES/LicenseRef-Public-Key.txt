Public key material

The files covered by this identifier are public OpenPGP keys: those of third
parties (upstream release signers), and the Runink River release key's public
half, which the image ships so that pacman can verify the [runink] repository.
They are redistributed unmodified, solely so that signatures can be verified.
The RIVER Authors claim no copyright in them and grant no license to them; any
rights belong to their respective owners. They may be redistributed as needed
for signature verification.
